# Image credits

All 40 photos in this folder were downloaded from Unsplash / Pexels
(free-to-use licenses: https://unsplash.com/license, https://www.pexels.com/license/).
They were resized to max 900 px (JPEG) to keep the repository small; the
full-size originals are not committed.

Filename prefix = intended category. Notes:
- `dog_09.jpg` is a husky, deliberately kept as a wolf look-alike hard negative.
- `dog_01.jpg` / `dog_06.jpg` are two similar golden-retriever-with-a-flower photos.

Individual photographer attributions were not recorded at download time. All images were obtained
from Unsplash/Pexels under their free-use licenses.
